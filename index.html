    Reminders

    Reminders are notifications from your own browser, about an hour before something is due, and only while this page is open. Nothing is sent to a server, and your assignments never leave this device. Your browser asks for permission first; you can turn reminders off here or in your browser's site settings.

    Import and backup

    Your list is saved only in this browser. Export a backup file to keep a copy, or import one to bring your list back or move it to another browser.

    You can also import a calendar (.ics) file exported from a calendar app. The file is read only in this browser and is never uploaded. Importing the same calendar again updates it without making duplicates.
